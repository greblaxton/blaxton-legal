BLAXTON CAPITAL

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Blaxton Capital LLC provides commercial and multi-family real estate advisory, acquisition, and asset management services in Massachusetts, and helps renters find apartments in the Boston area through Blaxton Capital Rentals. This Privacy Policy explains how Blaxton Capital LLC collects, uses, retains, and discloses information in connection with blaxtoncapital.com, direct-mail outreach, rental inquiries, and communications with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Contact information, including name, mailing address, email address, and phone number.
  • Information you provide when you call, leave a voicemail, text us, email us, or otherwise contact us, including the content of your communications.
  • Information you provide about an apartment search, including move date, budget, number of occupants, and rental application details.
  • Call recordings and voicemail recordings when you communicate with us by phone.
  • Information about a property and related ownership information from public sources, including public property records.
  • Information about your interactions with our website and communications, where available.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Identify and contact property owners in connection with commercial and multi-family real estate advisory, acquisition, and asset management services in Massachusetts.
  • Send direct mail to property owners of record inviting them to call or text us about selling their property.
  • Respond to calls, texts, emails, voicemails, and other inquiries.
  • Discuss a possible property sale or related services.
  • Help renters who contacted us find an apartment, including sending listings, arranging showings, and processing rental applications.
  • Maintain records of inquiries, communications, and property-related activity.
  • Operate, secure, and improve our business communications and website.
  • Comply with applicable law and protect our legal rights.

Mobile Information and Text Messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. All of the categories of sharing described in this Policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent.

Blaxton Capital LLC does not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Our service providers, listed below, process mobile numbers only to deliver our own messages on our behalf.

We text only people who have contacted us first. Our property line, (617) 315-6140, replies to property owners and others who call, text, or email us about a property. Our rental line, (617) 752-3618, texts renters who contacted us about renting, by writing to us directly about one of our own rental listings, calling or texting the rental line, or emailing us. We use mobile numbers to respond to inquiries, communicate about the inquiry, provide customer care, and administer opt-out requests. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Texting is optional.

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT. For help, reply HELP or INFO, call (617) 315-6140 (property line) or (617) 752-3618 (rental line), or email allen@blaxtoncapital.com. Full terms are in our SMS Terms of Service.

Service Providers

We use the following service providers to process information on our behalf:

  • Follow Up Boss CRM, to maintain customer and inquiry records.
  • Twilio, to support voice and text communications.
  • Google Workspace, to support business email and related business communications.

These service providers process information on our behalf and under our direction. They do not receive mobile phone numbers for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information to service providers described above when needed for them to perform services for us. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a legal claim or investigation.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. This section does not apply to text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, which we do not share with any third parties.

Data Retention

We retain personal information, including communications, call recordings, voicemail recordings, and property records, for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, to maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. We may delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable law.

Massachusetts and California Resident Rights

Massachusetts residents may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about them, subject to applicable law and verification of the request.

California residents may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act and the California Privacy Rights Act, including the right to request access to personal information, request deletion of personal information, request correction of inaccurate personal information, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Blaxton Capital LLC does not sell or share personal information as described above. We will verify requests as permitted by law before responding.

To submit a privacy request, email allen@blaxtoncapital.com with the subject line "Privacy Request" or write to Blaxton Capital LLC, 40 Old Cumberland Rd, Wrentham, MA 02093. Please describe your request and provide enough information for us to verify and respond to it.

Our services are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at allen@blaxtoncapital.com so we can address the request.

Changes to This Policy
