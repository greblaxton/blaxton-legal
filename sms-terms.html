Program Name and Purpose

The Blaxton Capital property inquiries SMS program supports property-owner inquiries about selling commercial and multi-family real estate in Massachusetts.

The Blaxton Capital Rentals SMS program, on our rental line (617) 752-3618, supports renters looking for apartments to rent in the Boston area. Its terms are set out in the Blaxton Capital Rentals section below.

How Consent Is Established

Blaxton Capital LLC sends direct mail to property owners of record. The mailer invites recipients to call, text, or email us about their property.

We send text messages only to our past clients and to people who have contacted us themselves by calling, texting, or emailing any Blaxton Capital phone number or email address, including (617) 315-6140 and our office line (617) 546-4711. We do not purchase, rent, or import phone lists for text messaging.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message volume is low and conversational. Messages are sent only to the people described above and relate to their inquiry or our work together. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting Out

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT. After opting out, you will no longer receive text messages from this program unless you initiate a new conversation and applicable consent requirements are met.

Help

For help, reply HELP or INFO, call (617) 315-6140, or email allen@blaxtoncapital.com.

Blaxton Capital Rentals

Our rental line, (617) 752-3618, texts only renters who contacted us first: people who wrote to us directly about one of our own rental listings, called or texted (617) 752-3618, or emailed us about renting, and asked us to get in touch. We do not purchase, rent, or import phone lists, and we never text anyone who has not contacted us.

Messages relate only to the renter’s apartment search: replies to their inquiry, questions about move date, budget, and roommates, listing links, showing times and reminders, and application steps. When roommates are searching together, we may text them as one group. A roommate is added to a group text only after they or a roommate share their number with us for that purpose. Some replies are prepared automatically by our software from the renter’s conversation.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop texts from the rental line, including group texts. For help, reply HELP or INFO, or call (617) 752-3618. Texting is optional and is not required to rent through us.
